# Privacy Policy

Peptide Dosage stores all data locally on your device, in a SQLite database
file. There is no account, no login, and no telemetry or analytics of any
kind — nothing about your usage, your protocols, or your dose history is
ever sent anywhere.

The one exception is the Literature Tracker feature: if you choose to look
up a PubMed ID, the app sends that ID to the National Library of Medicine's
public [NCBI E-utilities API](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK25497/)
to fetch the article's title, authors, and abstract. No other data (your
protocols, dose history, or profile names) is included in that request.

This is a research/educational tool, not a medical device, and is not
intended to provide medical advice.

Questions: open an issue at
[github.com/alinuxfan/peptide-dosage-app](https://github.com/alinuxfan/peptide-dosage-app/issues).
